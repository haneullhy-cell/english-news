The Longest Day of the Year

모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

June twenty-first is a big day because warm summer starts on this bright day. Folks in the north part of our earth have the longest day of light. Folks in the south part have the shortest day and winter starts for them.

Our big Earth goes around the bright Sun and leans to one side too. The top part of Earth leans to the Sun so it gets much light. This tilt makes long daylight for us and we call it the summer solstice. When the top part gets much sun, the bottom part stays cold and dark.

The Sun gets to its highest place in the sky at this time. Folks can celebrate this fun day from the early dawn to late warm night. The real time of the solstice comes when the Sun is way up high.

Long ago, folks built big tall stones in a big ring in old England. These heavy stones are called Stonehenge and they are four thousand years old now. The morning sun shines right through the rocks on this bright summer day. Many glad folks go there each year to see the great morning sunrise.

Folks in Sweden, Norway, and Finland have a big feast called Midsummer each year. They wear pretty crowns of fresh blooms and make big warm fires at night. Families make tall wooden poles with green leaves and sweet bright blooms on them. Kids and parents hold hands to dance and sing glad songs around the pole.

Across the cold blue sea, folks in Iceland celebrate the day with live songs. Folks who sing and dance come from many spots to play for three days. The sun does not set for three days, so it stays bright all night.

Folks in cold Alaska also have great fun during this long sunny summer day. In one town, players have a famous baseball game in the middle of night. This fun game began in nineteen zero six, and folks still love it now. They do not need big lamps because the midnight sun stays very bright all night.

The June solstice brings warm sunshine and happy outdoor parties across our big world. You can enjoy more sunlight with your dear family before the sun goes down. It is a great time to play outside and welcome the warm summer days.
