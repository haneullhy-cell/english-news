모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

On August 12, 2026, a total solar eclipse will happen. The Moon will move right between the Earth and the Sun. People in Europe will see the sky become dark during the day!

This special event is very rare. The Moon covers the whole Sun, so we can see its bright outer ring called the corona. It looks like a magical circle of light in the dark sky.

However, you must never look directly at the Sun. It can hurt your eyes very fast. You need special glasses to look at the eclipse safely.

If you do not have special glasses, do not worry! You can make a simple pinhole camera with paper. It shows the shape of the Sun safely on paper.
