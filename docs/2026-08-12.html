Why Horses in England Wear Soccer Socks

읽기 레벨 약 5.3 (AR 환산 추정) 원문 읽기 →

READ

Rescued horses and donkeys in England have a new look! They are now wearing bright red soccer socks from Arsenal FC, a famous soccer team.

Soccer players often cut off the feet of their socks. A clever man asked if the animal sanctuary could use the old top parts. The answer was a big yes!

Caretakers pull the stretchy socks over the animals' long legs. The socks protect them from biting flies and keep medical bandages in place.

The socks are also fun toys. Workers hide yummy treats like bananas inside them for the animals to find. Everyone loves this great idea!
