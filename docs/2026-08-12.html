A Big Music Show at the World Cup

The big World Cup soccer final will happen in July 2026. For the first time, there will be a big music concert during the game break. In the past, singers only sang before the tournament started or after it ended.

Many famous stars will sing at this special show. You can see Madonna, Shakira, and the famous group BTS. Cute puppets like Elmo and Kermit the Frog will also join the fun stage.

This show is not just for fun. It also helps children in need. The team wants to raise one hundred million dollars for schools and soccer programs. FIFA will give one dollar from every game ticket to help kids.

In soccer, the break usually lasts fifteen minutes. But this music show needs more time to build the stage. So, the break will be thirty minutes long. The music show itself is eleven minutes long.

Some people worry about the soccer players. If the break is too long, players' bodies can get cold and hurt easily. Players will get time to warm up again. If this show goes well, future World Cups may have big shows too.
