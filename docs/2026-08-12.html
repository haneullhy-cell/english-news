Every July, people in the United States celebrate National Ice Cream Month. This fun tradition started in 1984. President Ronald Reagan chose this month because many people love cold treats in summer.

Long ago, kings in China ate a frozen food made with milk and flour. Later, Arab chefs made a sweeter version with sugar. Ice cream came to America in 1744. President George Washington loved it so much that he spent a lot of money buying ice cream!

At first, ice cream was only for rich people because ice was hard to keep. But in 1851, the first big ice cream factory opened. After that, everyday people could buy and enjoy ice cream easily.

Today, many people love ice cream. Did you know your favorite flavor can show your personality? People who like vanilla often love adventures. Chocolate lovers are full of energy, and strawberry fans are very caring.

People even made giant ice cream to break a world record. In 2014, sculptors made the world’s largest single scoop in Wisconsin. It was taller than a person, and everyone shared it together!
