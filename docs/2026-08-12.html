Josh Kerr is a fast runner from Scotland. On July 18, 2026, he set a new world record. He ran one mile in 3 minutes and 42 seconds.

Before the race, Kerr worked very hard. He trained on a high mountain to make his body strong. He even slept in a special room.

About 60,000 people watched him run in London. Two other runners helped him stay fast. Near the end, Kerr ran super fast and passed everyone.

He broke a famous record from 1999. Kerr was very happy because his big dream came true. Now, he is a famous running hero!
