모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

On July 4, 2026, America will celebrate its 250th birthday. People will bury a special time capsule in Philadelphia. It will stay underground for 250 years. People will open it in 2276.

All states put fun things inside. Utah sent cards and coins. Arkansas sent a real diamond. California sent a small computer chip and a special picture of the future.

Scientists worked hard to protect these items from water. They made the capsule with strong steel. They used a round shape so it will not crack at the corners.

A big snake sculpture will mark the spot. The capsule holds pictures and a letter for future people. It shows our hope for the future.
