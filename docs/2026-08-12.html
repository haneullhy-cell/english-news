Look Up! Beautiful Shooting Stars in August

READ

Look up at the night sky! In mid-August, you can see many shooting stars. They are called the Perseids. The best night to watch them is August 12.

Shooting stars are not real stars. They are tiny rocks from space. When they hit Earth’s air very fast, they burn up and make bright lines of light.

These rocks come from a big comet named Swift-Tuttle. This comet travels around the sun. It leaves dust and rocks behind in space as it moves.

To see them, go to a dark place away from city lights. You do not need a telescope. Just use your eyes, be patient, and make a wish!
