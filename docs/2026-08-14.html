Tiny Fish That Can Climb High Waterfalls

모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Can you imagine a fish climbing a high rock wall? In Africa, a tiny fish can do this! It is called the shellear fish. It is very small, like a French fry.

These fish live in Africa. Every year, after big rains, they travel up a huge waterfall. The waterfall is fifteen meters tall. The water rushes down fast. Still, these little fish climb straight up the wet, slippery rock.

How do they climb without falling? Scientists looked closely at their bodies. They found tiny hooks on their front fins. These hooks grip the rock like Velcro. Their back fins push them upward.

Climbing is very hard work. To avoid strong water, the fish stay near the edge. They often stop and rest for an hour. Sometimes they slip and fall down. Then they must start over! A single climb can take ten hours.

Why do they go to the top? Scientists think they want better food and safer homes. Only young fish make this climb. Adult fish are too big and heavy to climb the wall.
