모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Did you see the soccer games? Many players wore pink shoes. It was not a mistake. Big shoe companies planned this together. Why did they choose pink?

The grass on the soccer field is very green. Pink is a very bright color. When pink shoes are on green grass, they are very easy to see. People watching on television can see the pink shoes clearly. The shoe companies want everyone to look at their shoes.

Soccer players wear different colors for their teams. They wear blue, red, yellow, or white shirts. But no team wears a lot of pink. So, the pink shoes look very special next to the team clothes. They stand out.

Bright colors can also help the players. When players wear bright pink shoes, they feel strong and fast. They want to play better. The bright color gives them good energy.

But there is one small problem. Before, fans knew their favorite players by their shoe colors. Now, many players wear the same pink shoes. Fans have to look at the numbers on their shirts to know who they are!
