모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

NASA has a spaceship named Psyche. It is on a very long trip in space. In May 2026, the spaceship flew very close to Mars. Mars is a big red planet. The spaceship used Mars to go faster. Mars helped push the spaceship without using extra fuel. This was a very smart move!

The spaceship has special electric engines. But they are a little weak. That is why it needed a big push from Mars. Now, it is flying very fast. Where is it going? It is flying to a special space rock. This rock is far away, between Mars and Jupiter. Its name is 16 Psyche.

Most space rocks are made of ice or normal rock. But 16 Psyche is very different. It is very big and full of metal. Scientists think it has a lot of iron. A long time ago, it was maybe the center of a baby planet. Big crashes broke the outside parts of the planet. Only the metal center was left.

The spaceship will reach the metal rock in August 2029. It will fly around it and look at it very closely. Earth has a metal center, too. But Earth's center is deep inside, so we cannot see it. By looking at this metal rock, scientists can learn how planets are made.
