How to Keep Your Pets Cool

모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Summer is here, and the sun is very hot! When we feel hot, our pets feel hot too. It is very important to keep dogs, cats, and other pets cool. First, never leave your pet in a car. Cars can get super hot very fast. It is very dangerous for them.

Try to keep your pets away from the hot sun. If you want to walk your dog, go early in the morning. You can also go late in the evening when it is cooler. Always touch the ground first. If the ground is too hot for your hand, it is too hot for your dog's paws!

Pets need lots of fresh drinking water. You can put ice cubes in their water bowl. This makes the water nice and cold. Make sure they have a cool place to rest in the shade. If you take them outside, you can put special pet lotion on their ears and nose.

Watch your cats so they do not get locked in hot rooms. If you have fish or birds, keep their cages away from the sun. What if you do not have a pet? You can still help! Put a bowl of clean water in your garden. Wild animals and birds can drink it and stay cool.

더운 날씨에 반려동물을 시원하게 해주는 방법

날씨가 더워지면 반려동물도 더위를 느낍니다. 뜨거운 차 안에 혼자 두지 말고, 산책은 시원한 아침이나 저녁에 해야 합니다. 또한 신선한 물과 그늘을 주고, 야생 동물을 위해 마당에 물을 놓아주는 것도 좋습니다.

1번B. In a car
기사 첫 번째 문단에 반려동물을 뜨거운 차 안에 절대 혼자 두면 안 된다고 나와 있어요.

기사에 땅이 강아지 발에 너무 뜨거운지 확인하려면 손으로 땅을 먼저 만져보라고 했어요.
3번B. Put ice cubes in the bowl
기사 세 번째 문단에 물그릇에 얼음을 넣으면 물이 차가워진다고 설명되어 있어요.
