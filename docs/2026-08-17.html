The Super Mario Galaxy Movie is a huge hit! It came out in April and made over 750 million dollars in less than a month. It is already the biggest movie of the year. People all over the world love watching Mario on the big screen.

This new movie takes Mario into space. Mario and his brother Luigi leave the Mushroom Kingdom. They travel across many colorful planets and star systems. Princess Peach joins them on their big adventure. Together, they must stop Bowser and Bowser Jr. from hurting the galaxy.

The movie is fast and very exciting. It has bright worlds and fun music. People who play the video games love seeing their favorite heroes. Even people who do not play games enjoy the fun story. Parents who played Mario when they were young now bring their kids to the theater.

Mario is not new. A game maker created this friendly plumber back in 1981. He started as a simple character in old arcade games. Now, more than forty years later, Mario is one of the most famous heroes in the world!
