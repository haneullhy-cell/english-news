A young soccer boy made big sports news in Mexico at the World Cup. His name is Gilberto Mora, and he played in a big game for his land. Many glad fans watched him run out onto the green grass when Mexico won the match.

Mora was only seventeen years old when he stepped out on the grass to play. He is now the youngest Mexican player who has ever played in a World Cup. A very old soccer record stood for ninety-six years before this boy broke it. He is also the youngest player at this big event out of all the teams.

His team was winning the game, so the head coach told him to go in. Mora stayed calm on the grass because he wanted to help his pals win today. He passed the ball fourteen times, and each clean pass went to his good teammate. The young boy did not lose the ball, so the next team could not score.

Mora loved soccer when he was small because his dad was also a pro star. His kind dad taught him how to kick and how to pass the round ball. He worked hard each day after school, so he got good at a young age. When he was only fifteen years old, he began to play in big pro games.

The boy kicked hard and scored ten goals in fifty-three games for his club team. The top team coaches saw his fast feet, so they asked him to join them. He helped his club win a bright gold cup when he was sixteen years old. Many sports fans in Mexico were glad because this young boy played so well.

Now the Mexican team will play their next game against a fast South Korea team. If Mexico wins this match, they can move into the next round of the cup. The coach wants to put Mora into the game again when his friends get tired. Fresh legs can run fast, so Mora can keep the ball in the mid field.

If Mora kicks a goal in the next game, he will make more sports news. He would be the second-youngest player to score a goal in this big World Cup. Lots of loud fans cheer for him because they trust in this young soccer star. Mora wants to do his best and bring great joy to all the Mexican folks.

Kids all over the world can watch Mora and dream of playing on big fields. With hard work and daily practice, young players can do big things like this teen. All the fans are glad to see what this brave Mexican soccer boy will do.
