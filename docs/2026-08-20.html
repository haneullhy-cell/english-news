A Famous T. Rex Sells For Big Money

모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

A huge T. rex skeleton named Gus was sold in New York for fifty million dollars. Many rich people wanted to buy it, so they fought with bids for ten minutes. The price went very high, and it broke the world record for dinosaur fossils. This big beast cost much more money than any fossil that was sold before.

Fossil hunters found this great big dinosaur on a cattle ranch in South Dakota. They were getting ready to go home, but they chose to check one more spot. In just a few hours, they found a small toe bone in the rocky dirt. The team was very glad because they knew it was a giant meat eater.

The crew spent three hot summers digging in the dirt to get all the bones. Then they spent three more years cleaning the bones and putting them together with care. They named the dinosaur Gus because that was the nickname of the ranch owner. Sadly, the kind ranch owner died while the team was digging up the bones.

Gus is twelve feet tall and thirty-eight feet long, so it is super big. The skeleton has one hundred and eighty-three bones, which is most of its body. The real head bone was so heavy that workers made a light fake head. They put the real skull inside a clear glass box for people to see.

Gus still has thirty belly ribs, and these thin bones are very hard to find. These thin bones often break fast, so finding them is a very rare treat. Gus also has bite marks and healed bones from fighting with other big dinosaurs. These old marks tell us that Gus was a strong fighter in its long life.

No one knows who bought Gus because this buyer wants to keep it a secret. Scientists are not sure where the giant skeleton will go in the years ahead. Some rich buyers in the past gave their fossils to big town museums to share. Many people really hope that the new owner will do the same good thing.

Many scientists worry when people pay so much cash for these old dinosaur bones. When fossils cost too much money, only rich people can buy and keep them. If fossils stay in private homes, scientists cannot study them to learn new facts. Kids and families will also miss the chance to see them in real life.

Dinosaurs lived long ago, but people still love them and want to learn more. Everyone hopes that Gus will go to a museum where all people can visit. Then kids and scientists can look at this famous giant king of dinosaurs.
