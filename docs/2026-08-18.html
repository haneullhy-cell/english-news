Great Sleep Tips for School Kids

모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

School is starting again, and waking up early can feel hard! Sleep is super important for your body and brain. When you sleep, your brain makes new connections and your body gets strong again. Doctors say children need 10 to 11 hours of sleep every night to feel fresh and happy.

How you spend your day also changes how you sleep. If you run around and play outside, you will feel ready to rest at night. But even if you feel tired during the day, try not to take naps! Day naps can confuse your body clock.

At night, you need to help your brain slow down. Put away your phone, tablet, and video games one hour before bedtime. Screen lights keep your eyes awake. Instead, you can read a quiet book, drink water, or take a warm shower.

Make your bedroom dark and cool so you can fall asleep quickly. Going to bed at the same time every day helps a lot. If you feel worried about school, talk to your mom or dad. Sharing your worries will help you sleep peacefully!
