모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Do you like fun films? Many kids love Disney films because they are full of magic and joy. But do you know how old Disney is? It is one hundred years old! That is a very long time, so let us learn how it all began.

A long time ago, in the year nineteen twenty three, two brothers had a big dream. Their names were Walt and Roy. They started a small place to make art, and they called it the Disney Brothers Cartoon Studio because they worked hard to make people smile.

Five years went by, and the brothers made a new film. It was in black and white, which means it had no colors at all. In this film, they showed a fun mouse to the world. His name was Mickey Mouse, and Minnie Mouse was there too.

The name of this first film was Steamboat Willie. People loved the funny mice, and they laughed when the mice moved and danced. Soon, Mickey became a big star. He is still very famous today, and you can see him on shirts and bags everywhere.

Now, Disney makes many new films with bright colors and great songs. You might know a film about a sweet mermaid. She lives deep in the sea and loves to sing, so many kids watch these films and feel very happy.

To celebrate one hundred years, people made a big quiz. A quiz is a game with questions that asks how much you know about these fun films. You can play it with your friends, so it is a great way to test your brain today.

1번C. One hundred years old
