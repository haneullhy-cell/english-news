Advice for Starting a Brand New School

모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

This week is the start of a new school term for many students. It is not just a new term, but a brand new school. Starting a new school can feel really scary at first. You might worry about making friends or finding your classroom. But remember, you are never alone! Many other kids feel exactly the same way.

Some older kids shared their top advice for the first days of school. They said you should pack your bag the night before. This helps you feel ready and relaxed in the morning. Also, do not forget to smile and say hello to new people. A simple smile is the best way to make a new friend.

In a new school, you will learn about many exciting things. You might watch interesting videos about nature and science. For example, you could learn about a lost turtle that traveled five thousand miles to return home. You might also learn what causes big wildfires and how we can stay safe from them.

School is not just about reading books. You can join fun clubs and do amazing projects with other students. Some kids even build electric cars together! Other students might practice sports, like sisters who hope to be famous golf stars in the future. There is always something fun to discover.

You will meet many different kinds of people. Some students might start school with a disability, but they still enjoy learning and playing. You might even meet someone who takes care of a dairy cow! Every student is special and brings something new to the class. Have a great time at your new school!

새로운 학교를 시작하는 학생들을 위한 조언

새 학기를 맞아 새로운 학교에 가는 것은 떨리지만, 여러분은 혼자가 아닙니다. 선배들은 아침에 여유를 가지기 위해 전날 가방을 미리 챙기고, 웃으며 인사하라고 조언합니다. 학교에서는 자연과 과학에 대해 배우고, 전기 자동차 만들기나 스포츠 같은 재미있는 활동을 하며 특별한 친구들을 만날 수 있습니다.
