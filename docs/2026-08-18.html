There are many fun news tales today that can bring a big smile to you. You can take a new Harry Potter quiz and find your very own magic spell. Some kids might get a spell that makes light, and some kids can fly high. Taking this short test is a fun way to share magic with your good friends.

There is also a great new clip that shows the magic world inside of Minecraft. Players can explore big castle rooms and build fun magic towers with all their friends. It looks very cool since top movie spots are built with small square toy blocks. Many kids love this game since they can make all the things they want to see.

Next, we can see great sea photos that won a big prize from top folks. These clear shots show bright blue water and pretty fish that swim near the rocks. Kids love looking at these pics since the wild sea has many secret hidden spots. The lens takes clear shots even when the deep sea is dark and very cold.

In sports news, two fast runners from Europe made great history on the big track. Their names are Amy and Dina, and they worked real hard before this big race. They ran faster than all the rest, so they felt glad and full of joy. Winning first place makes them feel proud of all their hard work each long day.

To end, a top film studio will celebrate fifty years of making fun clay films. Artists use soft clay to make funny sheep and dogs that can move and talk. Fans all over the world love these cute friends and their silly new fun adventure. Which of these great news tales would you like to read about first for today?
