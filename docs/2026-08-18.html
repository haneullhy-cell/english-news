Fun Adventures on a School Residential Trip

모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Have you ever slept away from home without your parents? Sometimes, kids go on a special school trip called a residential. It is a fun camp where you stay for a few nights. You go there with your classmates and teachers. It is a great opportunity to learn new things. You can make wonderful memories together with your friends.

On a residential trip, you can try many fun outdoor games. Children often go rock-climbing on big walls or mountains. It can feel scary, but it makes you feel very brave. You might also go canoeing in a river or lake. Paddling a boat with your friends is hard work, but it is lots of fun.

Hiking is another popular thing to do on these trips. You can walk through green woods and see nature. During these fun games, children learn important new skills. They learn how to work as a team and help each other. They also learn how to fix problems when things get hard.

A young girl named Sienna went on a residential trip. She traveled to a place called Cumbria with her school. Sienna had a great time outside and tried new challenges. She wanted to share her good experience with other kids. She even gave some helpful tips to make the trip awesome.

Going on a residential trip is a big adventure for young students. You might miss your family at night, but you will stay busy all day. You will eat meals with friends and sleep in big rooms together. If your school plans a trip like this, you should go and enjoy it!
