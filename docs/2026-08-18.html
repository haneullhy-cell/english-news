모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Today we have some fun news from all over the world. We will learn about strong queens, deep seas, fast runners, and clay art. Let us look at these cool stories together.

First, there is a fun quiz about the most powerful queens. A long time ago, some queens ruled very big lands. They were strong and smart, so they made good laws. They kept their people safe from bad things. You can take the quiz to learn their names.

Next, we have some amazing pictures of the sea. They are for a big photo prize in the year 2026. The photos show big blue whales, cute seals, and tiny fish. These pictures show us how beautiful the deep water is. They also tell us that we must keep the sea clean.

In sports news, two fast women made history. Their names are Amy Hunt and Dina Asher-Smith. They ran in a huge race in Europe and ran very fast. When they won, they said they were "over the moon." This means they were very happy. They trained hard every day to win.

Finally, a famous film maker is 50 years old today. The name of the group is Aardman Animations. They make funny films using clay. People move the clay a little bit, and then they take a photo. They do this thousands of times to make the clay move.

It takes a very long time to make these clay films. But kids and adults all love to watch them. News can teach us so many great things. We can learn about the past, nature, sports, and art. Which of these stories do you like the most?

오늘의 질문

어떤 뉴스가 가장 재미있다고 생각하나요? 그 이유는 무엇인가요?

아이가 클레이(찰흙) 애니메이션이 어떻게 만들어지는지 궁금해하면, 집에 있는 찰흙이나 장난감을 조금씩 움직이며 스마트폰으로 사진을 여러 장 찍어 보여주세요.

퀴즈 정답과 해설

문제정답과 해설
1번A. They made good laws
기사의 두 번째 문단에 '여왕들은 강하고 똑똑해서 좋은 법을 만들었다(they made good laws)'고 나와 있어요.
2번C. To be very happy
기사에 육상 선수들이 우승한 후 'over the moon'이라고 말했는데, 이는 매우 행복하다(very happy)는 뜻이라고 설명되어 있어요.
3번B. They move the clay a little bit and take photos
클레이 애니메이션은 찰흙을 조금씩 움직이면서 수천 번 사진을 찍어(move the clay a little bit, and then they take a photo) 만든다고 해요.
