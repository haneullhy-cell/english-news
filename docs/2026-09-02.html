Remembering Jackie, a Famous Eagle

모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Jackie was a well known bald eagle who lived in a green valley in California. Many folks around the world loved her because they watched her on live video. She had bright eyes and big brown wings that helped her fly up high.

Jackie was born in the year 2012, and she grew up in a big nest. Her eagle mom and dad brought her fresh fish until she grew strong to fly. She left the nest that spring and flew across the wide valley to explore nature.

Four years later, Jackie came back to the valley to build her own cozy home. People put a camera near her nest so all folks could see her each day. She lived with a male bird named Mr. B, and they stayed together with joy. They worked hard every day to keep their big tree nest safe and clean.

In the year 2018, another male eagle named Shadow came to the tall tree. Mr. B chose to leave, and Shadow soon became Jackie's new and good partner. Jackie and Shadow cared for their white eggs together through cold winter wind and snow.

Lots of folks watched the two eagles take turns sitting on their precious eggs. The sweet birds brought fresh fish back home to feed each other every morning. Fans felt great joy when they saw Jackie and Shadow protect their young chicks. They became one of the top pairs of wild birds in the whole world.

In the middle of July, Jackie got into a big fight with two other eagles. A kind rescuer found her on the ground because she was weak and hurt. They took her to a good pet clinic so doctors could help her fast. The doctors gave her clean air and blood, but she was still sick inside.

Jackie passed away in August when she was about fourteen years old and very weak. Many folks felt sad, but they still think of her sweet and brave life. Her doctors worked day and night, but they could not fix her bad illness. Jackie will always have a big place in the story of the valley.

Shadow went back to the tall nest, and he spends time with a new bird. Folks still watch the live camera each day to see what will take place. Jackie showed the whole world how great and strong wild birds can be. Her sweet tale will live on through the nest she called her home.
