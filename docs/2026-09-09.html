모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Route 66 is one of the most famous roads in America, and many folks love it today. It goes all the way from Chicago to Los Angeles across the wide open land. The long path is near two thousand and four hundred miles from end to end. Folks call this big road the Mother Road, and it will turn one hundred soon.

The famous road had its official start in November 1926 with a brand new name. It connected eight states and helped folks drive from town to town with great ease. Many parts were made of rough dirt, but workers paved the whole road by 1938. Drivers were glad that they could move across the big land faster than before.

Hard times came to America in 1929, and millions of poor folks lost their daily jobs. Big dust storms also hurt farms, so many hungry families had no food to eat. Thousands of poor families drove west on Route 66 to find new jobs in California. They packed their old cars with all they owned and drove down the long highway.

After a big world war ended, more families owned cars and sought out new spots. Folks drove on Route 66 for fun, while warm diners cooked hot meals for travelers. Bright gas stops and cool motels opened along the road to help the tired drivers. A trip on the long road became a fun adventure for moms, dads, and kids.

Over time, more folks bought fast cars, and the old two lane road was too small. The government built big new highways in 1956 that were both wider and faster to drive. Drivers chose the new fast highways, so fewer folks drove on the old road each year. Route 66 grew quiet since most travelers used the giant highways to get to far towns.

In 1985, the government took Route 66 off the official map of big roads in America. Yet folks did not forget this road, and small towns worked hard to save it then. Kind folks protected the old gas stops, motels, and road signs along the way. Guests from all parts of the world can still drive on the famous road today.

Now, towns across the eight states have big parties for its one hundredth birthday. Folks can see cool car shows, hear live tunes, and watch a new road film. Folks can walk down the old streets and buy fun gifts at the small shops. Even if it is old, the Mother Road still brings joy and adventure to all.
