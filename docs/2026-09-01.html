Ancient Desert City Found in Egypt

Helpers in Egypt found a very old town under the hot desert sand. This special town was built about one thousand and six hundred years ago. People lived there when big kings ruled the land long before our time. The desert kept the old homes safe, so they still look very clear.

Most desert towns grew with no plan, but this town was very different. Clever builders planned the streets neatly before they started to make the houses. Big roads went north to south, and small paths crossed east to west. The roads led to open spots where people met to talk and play.

High walls and tall watchtowers protected the families from bad winds and robbers. A very large church stood proudly right in the center of the town. People walked to this big church every week so they could pray together. It was the most important building for all the people in the town.

The houses were made from brown mud bricks that stayed cool all day. Workers found bread ovens and flat stone tools inside these sunny brick rooms. Families used the hard stones to grind grain into soft flour for baking. These simple things show us how moms and dads cooked their daily meals.

Scientists were very excited when they found two special homes in the town. One home belonged to a helper named Tisous who served in the church. The other home was a secret spot where neighbors prayed together long ago. They met in this small house before the big church was even built.

The team also picked up two hundred pieces of broken clay pots today. People did not have paper notebooks, so they wrote on these hard pots. They wrote short letters and shop lists using old words from long ago. These clay pieces tell us what people bought and sold every single day.

Diggers found small bronze coins and shiny gold coins under the dry dirt. The gold coins show the face of an old king named Constantius Two. Finding gold in a desert village means these farmers had a rich life. They traded their food with other faraway cities to buy nice shiny things.

This amazing discovery helps us understand how desert families lived in the past. Their great empire lasted for a thousand years across many warm blue seas. We can still learn many fun secrets from these old sandy mud houses.
