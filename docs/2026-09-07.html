Peter Cullen was a well known star who gave his voice to cartoon friends. Many folks knew him as the grand hero robot who was named Optimus Prime. He died in August when he was eighty-five years old with his sweet family. His family said that he went to sleep peacefully with the ones he loved.

Peter was born in Canada a long time ago in nineteen forty-one with his brothers. He went to a stage school since he loved to tell grand tales to folks. When he was done with school, he went to work on fun TV shows. He worked hard for long years and took part in more than two hundred shows.

As time went on, Peter chose to use his deep voice for cool film ads. In nineteen eighty-four, he went to try out for a new show on big robots. The hero of the tale was a brave leader who could turn into a red truck. Peter was glad, so he talked to his big brother Larry on the new job.

Larry was a brave soldier, and he gave Peter some very good advice that day. He said that real heroes do not yell or act mean like tough film stars. He told Peter to be strong enough to be gentle with folks near to him. Peter used that warm idea, and that was how the robot voice was born.

Peter was the voice of Optimus Prime for long years in shows and fun games. Kids, moms, and dads loved his deep voice as it felt like a wise guide. He made a big film on the robots in twenty twenty-three when he was old. Folks from all over the world loved him each time he spoke as the hero.

Peter also gave his voice to Eeyore, the cute gray donkey from Winnie the Pooh. He first played the sad small donkey in nineteen eighty-eight on a TV show. He loved to see young kids smile when they heard the slow donkey voice. The donkey was sad at times, but Peter made him sweet and loved by all.

Peter also made sounds for a huge ape named King Kong and a fast car. He was very talented since he could make all kinds of sounds with his mouth. His family hopes folks will keep in mind to be strong and gentle like him. Fans will keep his warm voice safe in their hearts for a long time.
