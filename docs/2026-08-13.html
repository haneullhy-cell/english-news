The Famous Piano Playing Octopus Retires

모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

A famous octopus named Tako is now retiring. Tako lives with his owner, Mattias Krantz, who is a YouTuber from Sweden. Mattias always wanted to teach an animal to play the piano. In 2025, he got Tako from a fish market.

Octopuses are very smart animals with eight arms. Each arm can think on its own. But teaching an octopus was not easy. Mattias had to make a special piano for Tako. Because Tako could not hear sound in the air, Mattias put a special speaker in the water.

Also, Tako pulled keys instead of pushing them down. When Tako played the right notes, a little elevator brought a live crab into the tank for him to eat! Mattias trained Tako for six months. Finally, Tako learned to play music with Mattias.

Now, Tako is getting old and tired. Octopuses do not live for a long time. Mattias turned off the cameras so Tako can rest peacefully in his favorite cave. People around the world will miss this clever little musician.
