모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Every year, many bright space rocks fall through the dark night sky like magic lights. One of the best sky shows is called the Perseid meteor shower by space experts. People really love this fun show because it happens every year on warm summer nights.

You can see about twelve fast space rocks each hour starting in the month of July. The best time to watch the sky is the night between August twelve and thirteen. During this peak time, you can see from fifty to one hundred rocks each hour. The moon will be dark, so the bright space rocks will be quite easy to spot.

People like to call them shooting stars, but they are not real stars in deep space. They are just small bits of dust and rock that move around the hot sun. When Earth moves into this dust path, the small rocks hit our air very fast. The rocks get very hot and burn up, making long lines of bright light.

A big space rock named Comet Swift-Tuttle left all this dust behind long ago. This huge icy rock takes one hundred and thirty-three years to go around the sun. It passed near Earth in the year nineteen ninety-two, and it will come back in twenty-one twenty-six. Since this comet is so very big, it left many small bits of space rock.

People named this shower after Perseus since the lights look like they come from that group. You can see these space rocks from many spots, but places up north have the best view. This star group sits very high in the north sky, so it is easy to find.

Some of these space rocks make big bursts of light that people call bright fireballs. These great fireballs can shine as bright as big planets like Venus and bright Jupiter. The huge comet left large rocks, and those big rocks make these super bright lights.

You do not need a big telescope or binoculars to watch this fun sky show. You only need your own two eyes and a dark place far away from city lights. Your eyes need about fifteen minutes to get used to the dark and see the lights. When you see a fast light in the sky, you can also make a good wish. It is a great summer night that you can enjoy with your loved ones tonight.
