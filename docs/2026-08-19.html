Elon Musk is now the richest person in the whole world, and he has a lot of wealth. He is the first person ever to have one trillion dollars, which is a huge sum. He got this much money on June 12, 2026, thanks to his big space company.

His space company is called SpaceX, and it builds very big rockets that fly high. People wanted to buy a part of SpaceX, so they paid big cash for it. The price went up fast on the first day, which made the company very rich.

Since he owns a big part of SpaceX, his own wealth grew very fast too. Experts say he now has more than one trillion and three hundred billion dollars. This is one trillion dollars more than the next richest person in the world. In fact, he has more cash than almost all of the lands on Earth.

Musk did not get rich in one day, and it took him many long years. When he was a young boy in South Africa, he liked computers very much. He learned how to write computer code all by himself when he was young. By the time he was twelve, he made a video game and sold it.

Later, he moved to the United States so that he could study in school. After he finished school, he started a computer company and sold it for cash. He used that money to help make a bank on the internet called PayPal. Another big company bought PayPal, and Musk got even more cash from that sale.

He did not keep the money, but he put it into two new companies. He put a lot of money into SpaceX and a car company called Tesla. But by the year 2008, both of his two new firms had no money. To make things worse, the first three rockets from SpaceX crashed and failed to work.

Musk did not give up, and he used his last cash to save them. Then, the fourth rocket from SpaceX went up into space, and it worked well. Because of this, a big space group gave his company a job to do.

Around the same time, his car company also got enough cash to make cars. His car company grew very big, and people bought many electric cars from him. The price of his car company went up, which made him rich over time. Now, his space company is also doing well, making him the first trillionaire today.
