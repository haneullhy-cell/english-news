모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Many folks in America think back to a very sad day in the year 2001. Bad men took four big planes when many folks were flying high that morning. The planes had a lot of fuel for their long trip across the land. Two planes hit two very tall tower buildings in New York City that day.

These two tall buildings were called the Twin Towers, and many folks worked inside them. The tall buildings caught on fire and fell down after the planes hit them. Many brave fire crews ran inside the buildings to help folks get out fast. Many police helpers also ran into each tower to guide folks down the stairs.

Another plane hit a big army building called the Pentagon near Washington, DC that day. The strong building was hurt, and many folks inside lost their lives that day. People across the whole land felt fear since they did not know what took place.

The fourth plane did not hit a town because brave folks on board fought back. They stopped the bad men, so the plane went down in a big grass field. These brave folks saved many lives, but all forty folks on that plane died.

Almost three thousand innocent folks lost their lives on that very sad September day. They were desk workers, police, fire crews, and families who were on the planes. People in America wept as one, but they also helped each other in kind ways. Good neighbors gave warm food and warm clothes to folks who needed quick help.

Now, folks can visit a calm park where the tall towers used to stand. There are two big water pools with the names of all who died there. A brand new tower stands there today, and folks call it the Freedom Tower. This bright building is very tall to show that the land is strong and free.

There is a quiet place in the field with forty wind chimes that sing. Each wind chime makes sweet sounds in the wind to honor every brave hero. Another calm park near the Pentagon has a bench for each life lost there. These bright benches shine in the dark of night to bring peace to all.

Every year on September eleventh, folks in America stop and think of these helpers. They call this day Patriot Day, and they put flags up in their towns. Folks learn to be brave and kind when bad things hit their good friends. We can think back to these good folks and help make our world safe.
