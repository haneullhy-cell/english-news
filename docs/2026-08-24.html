Very Old Potatoes Found in South America

모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Scientists in South America found two old potatoes that still look very good today.

A man named Dr. Valdez found them in the year two thousand twenty-four.

These amazing potatoes are about five hundred years old, and that is a very long time.

The scientists were digging in an old place when they found a small room.

They saw a broken clay pot under the dirt floor of the dark room.

When they looked inside the broken pot, they saw the two dry old potatoes.

People in this place ate a lot of potatoes for many thousands of years.

But fresh potatoes have a lot of water inside them, so they are very heavy.

Because they have so much water, they can go bad quickly in warm places.

The farmers wanted to keep their food for a long time without it going bad.

They left the potatoes outside on the cold ground during the freezing winter nights.

When the sun came up, the warm light made the cold potatoes soft again.

After the sun made the potatoes soft, the workers stepped on them with their feet.

Stepping on the potatoes helped to push all the water out of the food.

The farmers did this day and night until the potatoes were very dry and light.

These dry potatoes are called chuño, and they can last for many long years.

The dry food was very easy to carry because it did not weigh very much.

This helped feed the soldiers and workers when they did not have enough fresh food.

The people who made this food lived in a big group called the Inca Empire.

Their land went from the cold mountains all the way to the hot sandy deserts.

Having this dry food helped everyone stay strong and healthy across the big empire.

The dry potatoes were made high up in the cold mountains where the weather is freezing.

But the scientists found them near the hot beach, which is very far away.

They think animals called llamas carried the dry food all the way down the mountain.

Dr. Valdez says these old potatoes teach us a very important lesson about our food.

Today, many people throw away good food because they buy too much at the store.

We can learn from the people of the past and try to stop wasting food.
