모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Have you ever looked up at the night sky and seen the moon turn dark red? In late August, many folks around the world will get to see a rare moon. This big sky event is called a lunar eclipse by people who study the stars. It happens when our home world Earth blocks the bright light from the warm sun.

A lunar eclipse can only happen when the moon is round like a clean plate. During this great time, the sun, Earth, and the moon make one straight line. When Earth moves right between the sun and moon, it blocks all the sun rays. Earth then casts a dark shadow across the deep black space toward the moon.

Most times, a small eclipse only covers one tiny side of the white moon. It only hides a small spot of the bright moon under that dark shadow. This new eclipse will be huge because almost ninety-six percent of it will hide. As nearly all of the moon slips in the shadow, it turns to a blood moon.

You might ask why the dark moon does not just go away from sight. The quick answer is that bright light from the sun bends in our air. Sunlight bends when it travels through the thick ring of air around our Earth. This air clears out blue rays, but it lets red and orange light pass.

That red light travels through space and shines right onto the surface of the moon. This is the same reason why a sunset looks so red when day turns to night. Each sunset brings those warm tones when the big sun goes down in the west. If there is more dust or clouds in the air, the moon looks darker.

Folks living in North America and South America will have the best chance to watch. They can step outside late at night and stay up until midnight to look up. The clear sky will give them a great view of this rare red moon. Folks in Europe and Africa can only see the first part before morning light comes.

Watching a lunar eclipse is very safe, so you do not need dark glasses. It is quite safe for your eyes because the moon light is very soft. The main red show in the sky will last for over three whole hours. That gives folks lots of time to sit outside together and watch the moon.
