Two Special Days of the Jewish New Year

모르는 단어를 누르면 뜻이 나와요

Jewish people have a special new year, and this holiday is called Rosh Hashanah. It starts in September or October when folks meet to welcome the new year. The name means the head of the year, so it brings hope to all. Families meet at home, and they talk about the good days to come.

People go to a holy house called a synagogue, and they pray with good friends. They read from a big book, and they sing many sweet songs with joy. They also hear wise words because they want to learn how to live well. All the people smile because they know how the big world was made long ago.

A leader blows a special horn called a shofar, which makes very loud sounds. This horn is from a ram, and it makes four cool sounds for the crowd. Some sounds are long, but other sounds are very short and quick like taps. The horn sounds one hundred times so that each one will try to be good.

Sweet foods are also a big part of this day, because food brings hope. People dip crisp apples in sweet honey so their brand new year tastes sweet. They also share round bread, which shows that life goes on like a ring. Red seeds from big pomegranates remind folks to do many good deeds each day.

After this holiday, ten days begin, and people think about their past deeds. In this calm time, people recall each mistake that they made this past year. They say sorry to God and friends when they did things that were wrong. They also ask for forgiveness so that all can have a clean, fresh start.

The tenth day is called Yom Kippur, and it is the most holy day. On this day, grown folks fast and do not eat for twenty-five long hours. They wear clean white clothes, and they do not wear any leather shoes today. They spend the day in prayer, asking for good peace and for strong health.

An old tale tells about a great leader named Moses from a holy book. Moses went up a high hill to get rules carved on two stone tablets. When he saw people pray to a gold calf, he broke the hard stones. He was mad, but the people soon saw that they made a big mistake.

The people said they were truly sorry, and God gave them a fresh chance. Moses got new stones, and the people felt peace in their warm hearts again. So this day reminds us to forgive others and to be very kind today. It helps folks start fresh with lots of joy for the bright new year.
